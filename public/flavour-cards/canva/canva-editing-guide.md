# Canva editing guide — readable flavour cards

## Build the editable master

Create a **1080 × 1350 px** Canva design. Upload the SVG and the PNG. Use the PNG as a locked alignment reference, then recreate the text with individual Canva text boxes if Canva imports the SVG as one object.

The top **1080 × 680 px** area is the **ARTWORK / PHOTO PLACEHOLDER**. In Canva, make it a frame of the same dimensions, drag in your illustration or photo, then double-click to position the crop. Never change the frame size: this preserves the 4:5 card and its 27:17 artwork crop.

Suitable images include a flavour illustration, origin landscape, coffee cherries, brewing photograph or product shot. Use one confident image per card; avoid busy collages and keep its tones warm enough to sit with kraft packaging.

## Typography and hierarchy

Use **Cormorant Garamond Bold** for DRUNK, the coffee name and tasting notes. Use **Montserrat** for the remaining copy. Colours: Warm Ivory `#F7EFDD`, Deep Brown `#2E2019`, Kraft `#B98159`, and fine-rule brown `#A77B5A`.

At the 1080 × 1350 size, use these minimums:

- Coffee name: **88 px** — never reduce below 76 px.
- Tasting notes: **40 px** — never reduce below 34 px.
- DRUNK: 50 px; origin: 21 px; region / variety-process: 22 px.
- Description: 22 px; Best For: 21 px; brew suggestion: 19 px.

Keep the copy order exactly as shown: brand, coffee name, origin, region, variety/process, tasting notes, description, Best For, brew suggestion. If content is long, shorten the wording rather than shrinking the name, notes or description.

## Reuse and export

Duplicate the master before changing anything; retain one untouched page called **Flavour Card Master**. Replace the image in the top frame, then update the text boxes with a row from `coffee-flavour-card-bulk-create.csv`. For Bulk Create, connect all text columns to matching boxes; Artwork Theme remains a creative prompt for selecting the image manually.

Export Instagram as **PNG, 1080 × 1350 px**. For printed inserts, export **PDF Print** and ask the printer for the finished size, bleed and cut line. A 4 × 5 inch card needs at least 1200 × 1500 px at 300 ppi; add 3 mm bleed only when requested.
