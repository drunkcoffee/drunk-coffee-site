# Flavour card system notes

Flavour cards are not bag labels: a bag label identifies the coffee quickly; a flavour card has room for one expressive image and the sensory story. Use it for Instagram, website product visuals, bean-release announcements and printed bag inserts.

Keep every card consistent by preserving the 1080 × 1350 canvas, 680 px artwork frame, warm ivory lower panel, deep-brown type, DRUNK lockup and the fixed copy order. The image is the one major variable: swap it for a flavour illustration, coffee photo, origin scene, cherries or brewing image while retaining its crop frame.

Do not change the coffee-name, tasting-note or brand hierarchy. Do not add badges, extra borders, cold-grey text or unrelated fonts. A single warm editorial image plus generous ivory space will keep the series connected even when individual beans have very different flavour profiles.

Recommended outputs: Instagram 1080 × 1350 px PNG; website 1080 × 1350 px WebP/PNG; 4 × 5 inch inserts at 300 ppi or a printer-approved PDF Print file.
